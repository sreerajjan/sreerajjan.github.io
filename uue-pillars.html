UUE Pillars

Utility, Usability, Experience. Three lenses on why a screen isn’t converting — and, more usefully, three separate diagnostic questions to ask before touching anything. Most CX audits collapse all three into a single “user experience” verdict. UUE keeps them apart on purpose, because the fixes for each are almost never interchangeable.

Utility asks: does this screen let the user do the thing they came here to do at all? Usability asks: can they do it without friction, in the time and mental effort they’re willing to spend? Experience asks: does doing it leave them wanting to come back? A screen can pass one pillar and fail the other two — and the intervention is different every time.

The framework was built to score 20+ product surfaces at Bajaj Finance across personal loans, gold loans, business loans, home loans, and fixed deposits. The full write-up covers the 20-dimension underlying rubric, the persona weighting, and why screen-type matters more than most heuristic evaluations admit.
