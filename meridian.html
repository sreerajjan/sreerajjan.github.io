Meridian: building an intelligence layer for a 20M-user platform

Customer insight at a large NBFC lives in dozens of research decks, hundreds of survey reports, thousands of call recordings, and the collective memory of a handful of long-tenured researchers. Product teams reinvent the same insights every quarter because the last team's findings sat in a folder no one knew existed.

Meridian is my attempt to fix that. A capture-and-retrieval layer built on a five-field structure (What / Where / Why / Weight / Source), a four-layer tag stack, and a self-routing conversational agent over the research corpus. Prototype built in React with Microsoft Copilot Studio. Presented to the President and IT Head; currently being scoped for platform-wide adoption.

The full write-up covers the design decisions — why five fields and not ten, why tags over folders, why plain-language querying beats keyword search on this kind of corpus — and what I learned about shipping AI-native product inside a large regulated organisation.
